SOURCE POLICY

Public data, plainly labeled.

Meta’s public Ad Library is the verification source. This workspace does not ask for or use a Facebook login, client identity, or Meta API token.

  • One steady Tor route is optional; no circuit or IP rotation to bypass a block.
  • Rounded Meta counters keep the ~ marker.
  • Unknown, stale, failed, and inactive are distinct states.
  • Verification may wait when Meta throttles; a 100/min rate is not guaranteed.
